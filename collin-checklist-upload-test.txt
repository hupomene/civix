Collin County Commercial Tenant Improvement Checklist - CIVIX Upload Test

This is a test jurisdiction checklist document for CIVIX development.

Commercial tenant improvement projects may require updated plan sheets when the approved layout changes.

Revision Review Triggers:
- Restroom relocation may require updated architectural, accessibility, plumbing, mechanical, and electrical sheets.
- Interior partition relocation may require review of egress paths, corridor widths, lighting, receptacles, and mechanical diffuser coordination.
- New exterior doors may require review of landing, threshold, door hardware, exterior lighting, drainage, fire access, and exit discharge conditions.
- Multi-discipline changes should be submitted as a coordinated revision package.

Accessibility Review:
Restroom relocation should be reviewed for accessible route continuity, turning space, clear floor space, door maneuvering clearance, lavatory clearance, and threshold conditions.

Life Safety Review:
New doors and wall changes should be reviewed for exit access travel distance, egress width, exit signage, emergency lighting, door swing, occupant load path, and exit discharge coordination.

MEP Review:
Restroom relocation may affect sanitary routing, vent routing, domestic water, cleanout access, exhaust fan location, lighting, switches, receptacles, and fixture schedules.
