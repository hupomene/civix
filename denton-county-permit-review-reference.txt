Denton County, TX Permit Review Reference - CIVIX MVP Draft

This jurisdiction reference is used for preliminary CIVIX MVP testing for projects located in Denton County, Texas, including commercial renovation and remodeling projects.

Project location context:
- Projects in Lake Dallas, Texas may require confirmation of the applicable authority having jurisdiction.
- The project may involve Denton County, the City of Lake Dallas, fire marshal review, accessibility review, trade permit review, or other local permitting authority review.
- CIVIX should flag that final permit requirements must be confirmed with the applicable local authority.

General permit review considerations:
- Confirm the authority having jurisdiction for the project location.
- Confirm whether the project is reviewed by Denton County, the City of Lake Dallas, or another municipality.
- Verify applicable building code, fire code, accessibility, zoning, and trade permit requirements.
- For commercial renovation projects, confirm whether revised drawings, updated scope narrative, and affected trade sheets are required.

Design change review considerations:
- Changes to floor layout may affect architectural floor plans, egress plans, accessibility details, door schedules, and permit narratives.
- Changes involving exterior doors may require review of egress, accessibility, exterior elevations, threshold conditions, landing clearances, and door hardware.
- Changes involving restroom relocation may require review of plumbing plans, fixture locations, accessibility clearances, turning radius, grab bars, and fixture schedules.
- Changes involving interior walls may require confirmation of whether walls are load-bearing, fire-rated, tenant separation walls, or non-load-bearing partitions.
- Changes affecting mechanical, electrical, or plumbing systems may require updated MEP sheets and coordination with the permit reviewer.
- Changes affecting roof-mounted equipment may require review of mechanical plans, structural support, roof penetrations, and equipment schedules.

Potential affected documents:
- Cover sheet and permit narrative
- Existing and demolition floor plans
- Proposed floor plans
- Exterior elevations
- Life safety or egress plans
- Accessibility details
- Structural plans
- Mechanical plans
- Electrical plans
- Plumbing plans
- Door schedule
- Fixture schedule
- Energy code or envelope documentation, if applicable

MVP limitation:
This reference is a preliminary Denton County / Lake Dallas testing document for CIVIX. It should be replaced or supplemented with official jurisdiction documents, reviewer comments, adopted code references, and current permitting requirements.
