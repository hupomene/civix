Lake Dallas, TX Official Permit and Building Code Evidence

Source 1: Lake Dallas Permits Page
Official URL: https://www.lakedallas.com/665/Permits

The City of Lake Dallas Development Services Department receives, reviews, and issues most of the City's permits for both commercial and residential properties.

Structural alterations, additions, remodels, and work involving mechanical, plumbing, or electrical elements cannot be conducted without a permit issued by the City.

All permits are processed by the City's Permit Technician.

Any work commencing before a permit is issued is subject to permit fees being doubled.

Fire-related permits and inspections are processed by the Lake Cities Fire Department.

All contractors performing work are required to register with the City of Lake Dallas and provide required documentation.

Commercial Building Permits include structural alterations, additions, repairs, and remodels.

Source 2: Lake Dallas Building and Municipal Codes Page
Official URL: https://www.lakedallas.com/229/Building-Municipal-Codes

The City of Lake Dallas has adopted the Lake Dallas Municipal Code and International Building Code to establish minimum requirements to safeguard public health, safety, and general welfare.

Effective May 23, 2019, Lake Dallas adopted the following codes:
- International Building Code, 2018 Edition
- International Energy Conservation Code, 2018 Edition
- International Fire Code, 2018 Edition
- International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 Edition
- International Mechanical Code, 2018 Edition
- International Plumbing Code, 2018 Edition
- International Residential Code, 2018 Edition
- National Electrical Code, 2017 Edition, including NCTCOG and Lake Cities Fire Department amendments

The City's adopted codes address:
- Adequate light and ventilation
- Egress facilities
- Energy conservation
- Safety to life and property from fire and other hazards
- Sanitation
- Stability
- Structural strength

The City of Lake Dallas requires building permits when construction work is performed within City limits.

CIVIX Interpretation for Permit Review

For a commercial renovation or remodel project located at 5008 S Stemmons Fwy, Lake Dallas, Texas, CIVIX should treat Lake Dallas, TX as the primary authority having jurisdiction for city permit review.

Potential design changes that involve structural alterations, additions, repairs, remodels, mechanical work, plumbing work, electrical work, exterior doors, egress, accessibility, or fire-related systems should be flagged for possible permit impact.

Potential affected documents may include:
- Cover sheet
- Permit application narrative
- Architectural floor plans
- Demolition plans
- Life safety or egress plans
- Accessibility details
- Exterior elevations
- Door schedules
- Mechanical plans
- Electrical plans
- Plumbing plans
- Energy code documents
- Contractor registration documents
- Fire-related permit or inspection documentation, if applicable

CIVIX should note that fire-related permits and inspections may involve the Lake Cities Fire Department and that final applicability must be confirmed with the City of Lake Dallas Development Services Department.
